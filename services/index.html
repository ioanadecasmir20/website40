Frequently Asked Questions

Container haulage FAQs

What is container haulage?

Container haulage is the transportation of shipping containers between ports, terminals, warehouses, distribution centres and final delivery points, ensuring goods move safely and efficiently.

What areas does Dhillon Transport cover?

We provide nationwide container haulage services across the UK, regularly supporting collections from ports including Felixstowe, Tilbury and London Gateway, with deliveries across England, Scotland and Wales.

What types of containers can you transport?

We can support standard 20ft and 40ft containers, refrigerated containers, hazardous-goods movements and specialist loads, subject to job requirements and compliance checks.

Can I track my container?

Yes. Our transport operation uses vehicle tracking so customers can receive status updates throughout the movement of their shipment.
